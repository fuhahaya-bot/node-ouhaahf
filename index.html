Our Core Focus Areas

We tackle the biggest environmental challenges through focused, science-based initiatives around the globe.

Forest Conservation

Safeguarding ancient forests and accelerating reforestation efforts to capture carbon and protect watersheds.

Ocean & Water Security

Fighting plastic pollution, protecting marine life, and ensuring clean, safe water access for vulnerable communities.

Sustainable Energy Transition

Investing in and promoting community-scale renewable energy projects to reduce reliance on fossil fuels.
